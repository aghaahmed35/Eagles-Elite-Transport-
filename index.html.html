Policies

Terms & Conditions


📋 Seat Registration & Cancellation

A seat reservation fee of Rs. 1,000 is required to confirm your booking. This amount will be adjusted in your monthly fare and can be submitted via EasyPaisa or bank transfer.

Passengers wishing to discontinue must inform admin at least 10 days in advance. Failure to do so will result in the full fare being charged for that month.

⛽ Fuel Policy & AC Charges

An additional Rs. 500 will be charged for air conditioning during summer season (tentatively April–August). Monthly fare may increase or decrease based on current fuel prices (currently Rs. 272.15 petrol / Rs. 284.35 diesel).

💰 Fixed Monthly Fee Policy

The fee structure is based on 22 working days and is fixed per reserved seat. The same fee applies even if you use the service for fewer days. Extra days (Saturday/Sunday) are charged separately on a per-day basis. During exam periods, the full monthly fee applies regardless of individual exam days.

🚐 Missed Van Due to Driver's Negligence

If a van is missed due to the driver's fault, contact EETS Admin on WhatsApp immediately. If EETS is found responsible, an alternative travel arrangement will be provided at no additional cost.

🏛️ Government Holidays & Service Disruptions

The van is unavailable on government holidays and payments for these days are non-refundable. Services may be delayed, rescheduled, or cancelled due to extreme weather, strikes, or government restrictions. Passengers will be notified as soon as possible.

🚫 Passenger Conduct

Smoking, drinking, or any illegal activities inside the vehicle are strictly prohibited. Passengers must show respect to drivers, staff, and fellow travelers at all times. Any damage caused to the vehicle by a passenger will be charged accordingly.

📝 Discontinuation Policy

Members may discontinue the service for genuine personal reasons. However, discontinuation based on invalid excuses will require payment of the full month's fee. If a member has legitimate concerns about service quality, they must notify EETS management first. Should EETS fail to resolve concerns, the member may discontinue without penalty.
